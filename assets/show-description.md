## Short Description (≈50 words)

*For Apple Podcasts and Spotify summary fields, where space is tight.*

Most tech stories skip from "invention" to "winner." Backbone tells the messy middle — who resisted and why, what had to be built first, and what changed once the technology disappeared into the background. Hosted by Jeff Keltner and Cyrus Mistry. New episode every month.

---

## Long Description (≈150 words)

*For Transistor's "About" page, podcast detail views, and the show website.*

Every technology you depend on was once a curiosity that smart people thought would never work. Backbone is a deeply researched, narrative-driven podcast about how breakthroughs actually become infrastructure — the decades of resistance, the complementary technologies that had to exist first, the tipping point from novelty to inevitability, and the second-order consequences nobody predicted.

Each monthly episode follows one technology through its waves of diffusion: the human drama of the breakthrough, the people who fought it (whose arguments were often reasonable), and what changed once it became invisible. Then we run it through the Backbone Test — has it become infrastructure you only notice when it fails, and could we ever go back?

Hosted by Jeff Keltner and Cyrus Mistry. For people who want the story behind the story, not the highlight reel.
