## Overall Assessment

This is a strong draft — the best version of this episode so far. The Script Writer's rebuild honored the hosts' structural asks (story-first opening, lens-based division, By the Numbers at the end, a rigorous How It Works with Jeff owning "you don't make cold, you move heat," a genuine Q3 disagreement) and the research corrections are visible everywhere. What it still needed was exactly what the hosts flagged on the June draft, in subtler form: **47 residual echo handoffs** (paraphrased mirrors, "Four months." / "Four months.", the next speaker repeating a quoted phrase back), **31 spoken production cues** ("can I take this one," "this is my wheelhouse," "in our prep," "the story you said we couldn't cut," "the numbers land harder now," "and then stop, because I think we should stop"), a few **continuity seams** between chapters written in isolation (the ch02 bridge promised ch04's theme; the infant-mortality stat mixed denominators; the Kigali figure was stated three ways), **two chapters a hair over their word bands**, and a **tag palette that leaned a quarter on `[amused]`/`[laughs softly]`** with `[emphatic]` unused. All fixed below. The episode now runs 24,026 spoken words (about 107 minutes at release speed), every chapter inside its band, net 39 words shorter than the draft.
