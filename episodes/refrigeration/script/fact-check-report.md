# Fact-Check Report — Refrigeration (Checkpoint-2 regenerated 5-chapter script)

## Summary

I verified the full priority set the Editor and Script Writers flagged: 4 believability stats, 9 numbers, and 7 quotes — roughly 25 distinct claims, cross-checked against the in-repo research files first and then independent web sources (IEA, EPA, UNEP, WMO/NOAA, NobelPrize.org, Thatcher Foundation, US Census, Congress.gov, multiple journalism/academic sources).

**Headline: the script is in very good factual shape.** Every load-bearing number and quote either verified cleanly or was already responsibly hedged on air. The three known-bad believability stats ("107 opens a day," "most-touched object / more than the phone," "faster than the smartphone curve") are confirmed **absent** from all five chapters — none survived. Adoption is correctly anchored to radio/TV/automobile.

**One substantive change applied:** the AC-vs-Africa line in ch05. The dubious version ("AC by itself uses more electricity than the whole continent of Africa") was hedged-and-flagged on air, written to be droppable. Verification shows the claim is **real and sourced** — but the accurate framing is *US air conditioning ≈ all of Africa's total electricity* (IEA), not a vague global "AC by itself." Rather than cut a true and striking stat, I tightened it to the accurate US framing and flipped the on-air hedge from "somebody should check this" to "this checks out, here's the source." Two adjacent lines (Jeff's "Flag it, move on" and Cyrus's "even the conservative version") were updated to match, with no seam.

No fabricated quotes found. No incorrect numbers found. No unbelievable stat survived.

## Believability Flags (accuracy vs. "right for the show")

- **AC-vs-Africa (ch05):** The single stat on the list that both sounds made-up *and* is true. Resolved by stating the accurate US framing and naming the IEA on air, so a skeptical listener gets the source in the same breath as the number. This is the safest of the three options (reframe > hedge > cut) and preserves the gut-punch.
- **"Three-quarters of food through the cold chain" (ch05):** Borderline-high but verified (Twilley). The on-air "by one estimate" hedge is enough; no listener will reject "nearly three-quarters."
- **"A fifth of the world's electricity" (ch03, ch05):** Verified, believable, well-anchored ("stopped being an appliance, it's infrastructure"). No flag.
- **"~100,000 ozone molecules per chlorine atom" (ch04, ch05):** Striking but verified by EPA/UNEP/ACS and framed as catalytic — believable as told. No flag.
